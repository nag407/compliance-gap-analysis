NORTHWIND TECHNOLOGIES
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version 2.1 | Classification: Internal | Owner: Chief Information Security Officer

1. INTRODUCTION

1.1 Purpose and Scope
This Information Security Policy sets out the overall objectives to protect the information
resources of Northwind Technologies ("Northwind") and applies to all employees, contractors,
and third parties who access Northwind information systems. The policy is organized according
to the domain structure of the ISO/IEC 27001 standard. Its purpose is to establish the
mandatory security requirements that safeguard the confidentiality, integrity, and availability
of Northwind information assets. The scope of this policy covers all Northwind business units,
corporate functions, and the information systems that support them.

2. ORGANIZATION OF INFORMATION SECURITY

Responsibility for information security is allocated to defined roles. The Chief Information
Security Officer (CISO) owns this policy and is accountable for the information security program.
Business unit heads are responsible for ensuring security requirements are implemented within
their areas. Security responsibilities are integrated into project management, and all changes
to production systems are subject to a formal change-management process with documented approval.

3. HUMAN RESOURCE SECURITY

3.1 Security Awareness and Training
All personnel are provided with security awareness training as part of onboarding, before being
granted access to information systems, and annually thereafter. The training equips staff with
the knowledge and skills to recognize and handle common cybersecurity risks, including phishing,
social engineering, and safe handling of sensitive data. Completion of annual training is tracked
and is mandatory; access may be suspended for personnel who do not complete it. Privileged users
receive additional role-specific training appropriate to their responsibilities. Cybersecurity
expectations are also incorporated into personnel screening and the joiner-mover-leaver process,
including timely revocation of access upon termination.

4. ASSET MANAGEMENT

Northwind information assets are identified and recorded in an asset inventory maintained by the
IT function. Assets are assigned an owner and classified according to the Northwind data
classification scheme (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted). Classification determines the
handling requirements applied to each asset. Asset owners are responsible for ensuring their
assets are appropriately classified and protected.

5. ACCESS CONTROL

5.1 Access Management
Access to Northwind information resources is granted on the principles of least privilege and
need-to-know. Users are provisioned only the access required to perform their role, and
segregation of duties is enforced so that no single individual can perform conflicting sensitive
functions without oversight. User access rights are reviewed at least quarterly by the relevant
system and data owners, and any excessive or unused entitlements are removed. Access rights are
revoked promptly when a user changes role or leaves the organization.

5.2 Authentication
All user accounts must authenticate with a unique user ID and a strong password of at least
twelve (12) characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all administrative and
privileged accounts, for all remote access, and for access to systems handling Confidential or
Restricted data. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited except where explicitly approved and
individually accountable. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing and are never
transmitted or stored in clear text.

6. CRYPTOGRAPHY

Cryptographic controls are applied to protect the confidentiality and integrity of information.
Confidential and Restricted data at rest is encrypted using AES-256, and data in transit over
untrusted networks is protected using TLS 1.2 or higher. Cryptographic keys are managed through
their lifecycle by the IT function.

7. OPERATIONS SECURITY

7.1 Malware Protection and Software Control
Endpoint protection software is deployed on all Northwind systems and kept current. To prevent
the installation and execution of unauthorized software, Northwind enforces application control:
standard users do not have administrative rights on their endpoints, only approved software from
the managed software catalog may be installed, and application allowlisting is applied on servers
handling Restricted data. Attempts to install or run unapproved software are blocked and logged.

7.2 Backups
Backups of critical data and systems are created on a defined schedule. Backup data is encrypted,
access to backups is restricted, and copies are stored in a separate location from the source
systems. The integrity and restorability of backups is verified through restore testing performed
at least quarterly, and the results of restore tests are recorded.

7.3 Logging and Monitoring
Security-relevant events across Northwind computing hardware, software, and runtime environments
are logged to a centralized logging platform. Endpoint detection and response (EDR) tooling and
network monitoring are used to detect potentially adverse events, such as malware execution,
anomalous access, and unauthorized configuration changes. Logs are retained for a minimum of
ninety (90) days and reviewed for indicators of compromise.

8. COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY

Communication networks are protected through segmentation, firewalls, and controlled network
boundaries. Information transferred with external parties uses approved, encrypted methods.

9. INFORMATION SECURITY INCIDENT MANAGEMENT

Suspected information security incidents must be reported promptly to the Security Operations team
through the established reporting channel. Reported events are triaged, and confirmed incidents are
managed through a defined incident-response process covering containment, eradication, and recovery.
Incident information is communicated to relevant internal teams, and where legal or regulatory
obligations apply, to external authorities. Lessons learned from significant incidents are captured
to reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

10. BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Northwind maintains business continuity arrangements to sustain critical services during adverse
situations. Continuity plans address the recovery of critical information systems, and the
continuity of information security controls is preserved during disruption.

11. RISK MANAGEMENT

Northwind operates an information security risk management process. Risk assessments are conducted
periodically to identify risks to Northwind information assets, and identified risks are recorded
in a risk register and assigned owners for treatment. Risk treatment decisions are documented.

12. COMPLIANCE

Northwind information resources are managed in a manner that meets applicable legal, statutory,
regulatory, and contractual obligations, as well as internal policies and procedures. Compliance
with this policy is mandatory, and violations may result in disciplinary action.

END OF POLICY
